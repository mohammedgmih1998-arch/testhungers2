Shipping & Refund Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Shipping

  • Processing time: orders are processed and handed to the carrier within 1 business day.
  • Delivery time: typically 7–12 business days, depending on your location.
  • Tracking: you'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.
  • Cost: shipping is free on every order.

If your tracking hasn't updated for more than 10 days, email us and we'll investigate with the carrier.

30-day money-back guarantee

If you're not satisfied with your hangers for any reason, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we'll refund your order in full. To request a refund:

  • Email support@yourdomain.com with your order number.
  • Tell us briefly what went wrong (this helps us improve — it doesn't affect your refund).
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval.

Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, email us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we'll send a free replacement or a full refund — your choice. You don't need to ship anything back.

Order cancellations

You can cancel for a full refund any time before your order ships. Once it has shipped, the 30-day guarantee above applies instead.

Contact

All shipping and refund questions: support@yourdomain.com. We reply within 24 hours on business days.
