Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Owlko ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you visit or make a purchase from this website.

Information we collect

  • Order information: your name, shipping address, email address, and phone number, collected when you place an order so we can fulfil and deliver it.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our third-party payment processor. We never see or store your full card number — payment details are handled entirely on the processor's secure systems.
  • Device information: like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical data (browser type, pages visited) to keep the site running and secure.

How we use your information

  • To process and ship your order and send order confirmations and tracking updates.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as tax and accounting rules.

Sharing your information

We share order details only with the parties needed to deliver your purchase: our payment processor, our fulfilment partner who packs and ships your order, and the shipping carrier. We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

We keep order records for as long as required for accounting and legal purposes, then delete or anonymize them.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you at any time by emailing us at support@owlko.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@owlko.com.
